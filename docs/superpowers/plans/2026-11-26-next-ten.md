# The Next Ten — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Ten improvements the owner approved on 2026-11-26 ("Let's go. Let's start building"), in two waves.
Wave 1 (independent, in parallel): 1 lighter and faster, 2 real limbs, 3 sound pass, 9 a night city, 10 share
moments. Wave 2 (after wave 1 ships, builds on the limbs): 4 two-player cricket, 5 doing things together, 6 hangout
counts on the map, 7 collections with rewards, 8 Holi and Gangaur.

**Architecture:** Builds on main 74d7452 (seven districts, 14 stamps, 13 daily-goal kinds, emotes protocol; migrations
0001–0008, 0010, 0011). Wave 1 is client-only. Wave 2 touches the Worker (Free plan: small, rate-limited, nothing
stored unless a migration 0012+ says so). Deploy order: migrations by hand, Worker, then the `pages` workflow.
Roll back the site first.

## Global Constraints

- Phones first (360×740); `hud.spec.ts` bounds hold; ≥ 10 px slack for CI's wider fonts; buttons ≥ 44 px; and since
  2026-11-26 every new HUD control is also checked on a desktop (no joystick: `tests/desktop.spec.ts`).
- Bilingual copy (Hinglish default + English) in `strings.ts`: original, warm, gender-inclusive, respectful.
- No per-frame allocations; instanced/merged meshes; dispose on district change; `prefers-reduced-motion`; tier 0
  thinner; sounds follow `gg-sound` and pause in hidden tabs; test hooks only behind `VITE_TEST_HOOKS`.
- Main bundle: 230 kB gzip today, limit 250. Task 1 brings it down; every other task must add ≤ 2 kB to the main
  chunk (lazy-load the rest) and say its number.
- Privacy: ghosts, hidden and blocked people never shown, counted per person, notified or reachable; nothing new
  names or locates a person; a shared picture carries no id and no name unless the person opts in.
- Old pages and old Workers must not break: protocol changes additive, stamps and goal kinds append-only.
- Gate: `npm run lint && npm test && VITE_API_URL=https://api.gulabi-gali.manishbisht.me npm run build -w frontend && npm run check:test-hooks`, plus the touched e2e specs; the controller runs the full suite in the CI image.

## Review Focus

   and test that depends on the old body.

## Wave 1

### Task 1: Lighter and faster

- Lazy-load touchables (+ `audio/touch.ts`) and Swaad's text/list/card out of the main chunk; move cricket's and the
  shows' HUD copy into their lazy chunks. Target: main ≤ 222 kB gzip.
- Touchables: pump water and diya flames not drawn unless running/lit; fold the wing material into a shared one.
- Start a clearly weak phone at tier 1 (e.g. `deviceMemory ≤ 2` or `hardwareConcurrency ≤ 4` with a small screen),
  still free to step either way; a test for the rule; numbers in docs/device-performance.md.

### Task 2: Real limbs for avatars

- The player, other people and NPCs get separate arms and legs (still low-poly, still one or few instanced draw
  calls for the crowd): a walk cycle, a jog cycle, idle sway, sitting on a bench/tapri/ground, and real poses for
  the five emotes (ghoomar-like turn with arms, clap, sit, selfie arm, wave), the cricket swing arm, and a hand that
  can hold a prop (a kulhad after "Chakho").
- Every existing look option (the dressing room) still works; seated people, auto drivers, the procession and the
  rooftop kite-flyers keep working (they may keep their own rigs); postcards and OG pictures unaffected (re-make an
  OG picture only if the player is in it: they are not).
- Tier 0: limbs may be stiff (no cycle); reduced motion: no sway, poses held.

### Task 3: Sound pass

- One mix: a small master bus with sensible levels for ambience, the bell, pump, pigeons, purr, cricket, shows,
  horn, metro, kite fight; nothing clips when several play; ducking consistent.
- Add: soft footsteps (surface-aware if cheap: road / stone / sand), a dog's friendly bark on "Pyaar karo", night
  crickets after sunset, a tapri's kettle hiss near stalls.
- A dev-only "sound board" page behind the test-hooks build to audition every sound, and a short written review of
  each show tune (notes, range, loudness) with fixes where one is harsh. All synthesized, no audio files.

### Task 9: A night city

- After sunset the districts change character: lit shop signs and windows (emissive, cheap, no real lights beyond a
  few), string bulbs over one food lane per district that has tapris, the diya stands glowing, fewer NPCs walking
  and some sitting, a night-only food lane ("raat ka bazaar") in the Walled City and MI Road where two Swaad dishes
  are served only at night (data only: keep the list finishable by day).
- A few things only after dark: fireflies at Nahargarh and Galta Ji (instanced points), a night watchman NPC with a
  lathi and a line, the Hawa Mahal and Albert Hall lit up. Tier 0: signs only.

### Task 10: A share moment for everything

- Cards in the existing 1080×1920 style (`ui/cards.ts`, `useShareFlow`): a cricket score card (runs, sixes, "Naya
  record!"), a "Swaad n/8" card, a daily-goal streak card. Same privacy rules as the Darshan card (no id; a first
  name only when a resident ticks it).
- "Share karo" where each moment happens (innings over, list finished or any time from its tab, streak milestone).
- A short clip is OUT of scope this round (MediaRecorder support on iOS is unreliable): a still card only.
